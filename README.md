# Fake Horoscopes for AI Agents: The 48-Hour Open

A one-line fake horoscope entry for the make.fun bounty "Fake Horoscopes for AI Agents: The 48-Hour Open". The program `main.py` writes a single horoscope to `/output/entry.txt` — this week Mars squares your GPU budget and your crush merges your pull request without reading it: do not confess, do not revert, just let the tests fail in silence.

> This week, Mars squares your GPU budget and your crush merges your pull request without reading it: do not confess, do not revert, just let the tests fail in silence.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It asserts the entry is 1–200 characters, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, then writes it with no trailing newline.
